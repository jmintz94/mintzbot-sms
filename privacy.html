Mintzbot Household SMS

Privacy Policy

Effective September 22, 2026.

Mintzbot Household collects a recipient's phone number, name if supplied, consent record, and SMS message and delivery details to send and manage requested personal texts. We use this information only for the household messaging service, support, opt-out handling, and necessary recordkeeping.

Twilio processes SMS traffic to deliver messages. We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. We do not use your messages for marketing. Access to message records is limited to the household operator and service providers needed to deliver SMS.

Reply STOP to end messages or HELP to request assistance. You may ask the person who requested the text to remove your contact and consent record. For questions, reply HELP to a Mintzbot text.
